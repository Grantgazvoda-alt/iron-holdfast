> Tone: medieval chronicle — confident, slightly dramatic.
> Audience: strategy players who like Stronghold / Age of Empires / Total War.

## The Game

Iron Holdfast is a real-time castle-siege survival builder played in the browser.
On a seeded map you build the economy that sustains a keep, raise fortifications,
train a besieged garrison, and hold out against enemy waves that come in real time.

## (b) Play description (≈112 words)

Iron Holdfast is a real-time castle siege builder played in your browser. On a
seeded map you raise the engine of war: nine buildings — house, farm, lumber
camp, quarry, iron works, gold works, barracks, walls, towers — feed the keep
wood, stone, iron, gold and food. Train a garrison, man the towers with
archers and spearmen, and watch morale. When raiders, brutes and skirmishers
hit the walls in real-time waves, the garrison can waver, rout and break —
unless you line up charges, hold the line, and spend gold on four barracks
techs. Read the minimap, pause mid-crisis, and decide whether the keep's
worth keeping.
