# Kingdom Campaign — Narrative Wave
North/pitch frame: every beat is a consequence, every outcome deterministic & legible, every cost shown before you act.

## 1. Three-Act Crystal
**ACT I — SETUP: "The Crown Sleeps"**
- Kingdom **Falden** fractures at the Thoro-Keep succession. Player enters as a landed marshal — win-state is heir, crown, or ruin; every first move is a territorial bet.
- Settlements: **Redhollow** (south garrison), **Westmere** (freighted market), **Pontry** (royal bridge). Lords: **Sir Ondreth** (loyalist), **Dame Velmarsh** (neutral), **Provost Sable** (old-money rival).
- Consequence: first conquest locks Act 2's enemy. Determinacy: each settlement shows liege, bonus, cost before marching. Legibility: win-state reads as a path.

**ACT II — ESCALATION: "The Hollow Charter"**
- **Veyburn** marsh treaty replaces war with charter-negotiation — win a settlement by army OR by charter. Supply thins; one rival (Provost Sable) refuses charters and opens a second front.
- Named event: **Hollow Crossing at Westmere** — control decides which Act 3 endcard.
- Consequence: war locks out charter and vice versa. Determinacy: both winnable, both stated. Legibility: map redraws into a "conquest vs charter" axis.

**ACT III — PAYOFF: "The Coronal"**
- Win-state: **Crown of Falden** — reached only via the climax siege. Hold Redhollow + Pontry + win **The Last Candle** (siege) or **Charter Court** → crown; otherwise *palace-in-exile* (retention-safe end).
- Deterministic end-state, named, legible, repeatable — no hidden RNG gate.

## 2. Scripted beats (questable)
- **Beat 1 — Green Siege at Redhollow:** rescue garrison from Sir Ondreth's siege line. Effect: Redhollow flips to vassal, unlocks southern deploy lane. Loss = deeper bolt strikes weaken south supply.
- **Beat 2 — Hollow Charter at Westmere:** negotiate or axe. Effect: charter-win nets +gold +retention +gates into Veyburn; war-win mints salt hub but forfeits the quiet treaty.
- **Beat 3 — Last Candle at Pontry:** final assault on Provost Sable. Effect: victory unlocks crown-grounds squads + "Third Lance" morale buff across maps; failure leaves a closed-loss state (no false retry).

## 3. Faction-voice bar (2 lines each)
- **Crown of Falden (royalist):** "The river answers to one law — ours. March, and the map shall speak your name."
- **House Veyburn (charter-marsh):** "We do not trade blood; we trade terms. A pen writes further than a siege."
- **Pontry Provost (old-money):** "Gold is the only crown that never rusts. Yours is welcome — at my price."

Guardrails: all map consequences legible pre-script; determinacy at every branch; win/loss writes to save; beats repeatable, no soft-lock.
