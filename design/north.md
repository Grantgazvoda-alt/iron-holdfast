# NORTH — Iron Holdfast

## Vision
You tell a friend: "I lead a company of grim soldiers in a brutal dark-fantasy kingdom. Every choice — when to march, who to raid, whether to feed the troops or let them desert — actually matters. I hold my keep through endless siege waves, and when I survive, it's because I planned, not because I got lucky."

## Pillars
1. **Consequence.** Every call bends the world.
   Rule: no decision is cosmetic — supply, morale, and loyalty accumulate and bite back.
2. **Determinism.** The sim runs on fixed rules; same input, same output.
   Rule: players can always reason their way to the right move.
3. **Legibility.** A glance at the screen tells you who's winning and why.
   Rule: never make the player hunt for information.

## Vertical Slice
Goal: one loop, end to end, playable now.
Open-world layer first — march to sites, raid for supplies, ration them, and watch desertion pressure build before it breaks the company. Between tiles, Holdfast defense waves keep the keep. Premium tactical layer sits on top: commander abilities and tactical commands that cost a scarce resource.
Ship when march → raid → supply → defense feels tense, fair, and reads on one screen.

## No-Go
- No placeholder buttons — every control is live and does something real.
- No hidden randomness — the simulation is deterministic, always.
- No sprawl — everything that matters must fit and read on one screen.
